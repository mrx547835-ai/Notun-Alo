Sylhet Tea Gardens Report Record Winter Harvest Amid Favorable Weather

By Tariqul Islam • Nov 23, 2026 • 1 min read
Facebook WhatsApp X

Tea estates across Sylhet and Moulvibazar have recorded a 14 percent surge in late-season leaf production following balanced rainfall.

Tea planters and industry officials in the Sylhet region expressed strong optimism today as auction prices and export inquiries climbed ahead of the year-end season. According to local estate managers in Sreemangal and Malnichhara, consistent morning mist and moderate daytime temperatures helped preserve leaf quality across major gardens.

Workers' cooperatives also noted improved output bonuses this quarter, while transport associations confirmed smooth delivery of processed tea consignments from Sylhet to the Chattogram auction center.

Experts believe sustained investment in shade-tree management and modern irrigation will further strengthen Sylhet's position as Bangladesh's premier tea-producing hub.
