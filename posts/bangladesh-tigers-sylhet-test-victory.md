A clinical pace bowling display and a composed fourth-innings chase handed Bangladesh a memorable four-wicket win in Sylhet.

Roared on by a packed crowd at the Sylhet International Cricket Stadium, the home side chased down 218 runs on the final afternoon with four wickets to spare. Openers weathered early swing under overcast skies before the middle order anchored the innings with crisp boundaries through the covers.

Earlier, the pace attack dismantled the visitors' second innings for 164, making full use of the lively Sylhet surface. The captain praised the ground staff for producing a balanced pitch that rewarded both disciplined bowling and positive stroke play.

The squad will now travel south for the second match of the series next week.
