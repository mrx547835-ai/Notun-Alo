Lawmakers and civic representatives gathered in Dhaka to discuss faster e-services and transparent municipal budgeting for district councils.

During a day-long policy dialogue held on Sunday, representatives from major administrative divisions emphasized expanding citizen-facing portals at the union and upazila levels. Speakers highlighted that automated land records and online birth registration systems have already cut waiting times significantly in several districts.

Opposition and treasury members alike called for stronger data privacy protections and independent audits before rolling out the next phase of public service automation in early 2027.

A joint committee is expected to submit final recommendations to the cabinet secretariat within three weeks.
