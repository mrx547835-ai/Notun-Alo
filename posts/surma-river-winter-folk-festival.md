Traditional baul melodies, poetry recitations, and local book stalls lit up the banks of the Surma River as the winter cultural festival opened.

Visitors of all ages filled the open-air amphitheater near Keane Bridge from late afternoon as folk troupes from Sunamganj, Habiganj, and Sylhet performed classic compositions of Hason Raja and Shah Abdul Karim.

Alongside the musical stage, more than forty regional publishers and craft artisans set up pavilions showcasing Manipuri handloom textiles, cane crafts, and newly released Bengali fiction.

Organizers confirmed that the festival will continue through Saturday evening with special acoustic sessions and youth drama performances.
